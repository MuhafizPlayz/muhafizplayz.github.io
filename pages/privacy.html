Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is involved when you use this website. It is a general template; the site operator should review it and adapt it to local law.

Information we collect

  • Visitors: this site does not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal information. Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and the database provider (Supabase) may process technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and content.
  • Administrators: administrator accounts use an email address and password handled by Supabase Authentication. A session is stored in your browser so you stay signed in.

Cookies and advertising

The site itself uses browser storage only for administrator sign-in. If the operator enables advertising, the ad network may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads. Please read that network’s own privacy policy.

External links

Download or watch links may lead to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings. To ask a question about your data, use the Contact page.
