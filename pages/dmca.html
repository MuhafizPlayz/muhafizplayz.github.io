DMCA / Copyright

We respect the rights of creators and copyright owners. This site is meant to list only content the operator owns, created, licensed, or is otherwise permitted to distribute.

How to send a copyright notice

If you are the copyright owner (or authorized to act for the owner) and believe material on this site infringes your rights, send a notice to the email address on our Contact page that includes:

  • Your full name, address, phone number and email.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you say was infringed.
  • The exact web address (URL) of the page or link on this site.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

What happens next

We review complete notices promptly and remove or disable access to material that appears to infringe. If you believe material was removed by mistake, you may send a counter-notice to the same address explaining why the removal was an error.

Submitting a knowingly false notice may have legal consequences.
